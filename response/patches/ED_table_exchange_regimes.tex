\caption{\textbf{Exchange regimes as ratios of genealogical depths.} For each regime, $R$ is the
coalescence time of two non-homologous subtelomeric copies divided by the coalescence time of two
alleles in one population pool. $R = 1$ is the obligate case, where mixing is complete and the two
loci behave as a single pool; the obligate coalescence time of 62,500 generations ($1.25$\,Myr) is set
by the population's genealogy, not by the crossover rate, which is why crossovers cannot be counted as
events. The last column is the mixing intensity relative to the obligate case,
$1/(R-1) = 4N_e m$, under coalescence with migration. Times assume $1.2\times10^{-8}$ substitutions per
base per generation and are \\emph{upper bounds}: both GC-biased gene conversion in this GC-rich
compartment and the elevated paternal-germline rate raise the effective substitution rate and shorten
the true times. $R$ itself is unaffected by that uncertainty, because numerator and denominator are
measured in the same sequence and cancel the rate.}
