R2. "The chr5q/chr1p candidate recurs in two generations (PAN027 paternal and
    PAN028); it happens twice, so there must be some problem with it."
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STATUS: OPEN — but the premise is wrong: the second occurrence is the
TRANSMITTED copy of the first, not an independent repeat. Evidence below.
(This is the Fig5 autosomal candidate the reviewer means — NOT chr9q/chr3q;
see R3 for that one. Fig5E also highlights a chr21p/chr4p run, whose chr4
partner is pericentromeric, not subtelomeric — lower priority.)

The two observations (sweepga f32 10:10 class-winner scan, whole-genome):

  G2: PAN027 paternal hap2 vs PAN011 — chr5q 182.052-182.080 Mb (28 kb),
      best inter-chr match PAN011 chr1 hap2, identity ~0.998 vs ~0.995 for
      the same-chr (chr5) match (narrow margin, ~0.003). Fig5B/C candidate.
      Flagger status: the 28 kb tract is flagger/problematic-NEGATIVE.
      (nearest flagger call: 182.112-182.144 Mb Col,COLLAPSE_OTHER — just
      distal of the tract)

  G3: PAN028 maternal hap1 vs PAN027 — chr5q 182.984-183.038 Mb (~54 kb
      chr1 h2 run, identities 0.9985-1.0 in f32 windows) plus a tail
      (183.09-183.11 Mb) that DOES fall inside a flagger Col,MISJOIN region
      (183.072-183.118583). The main 54 kb run is flagger-negative.
      Fig5E candidate.

What the sequence data show (wfmash v0.24.2-16-g4a35bfc5, -p 90, direct
test on the v1.1 polished assemblies, 2026-10-15):

  - PAN028's 56 kb chr5q region (182.984-183.040 Mb) maps to PAN027's
    PATERNAL chr5q (181.9-182.15 Mb window) in 4 split blocks at
    97.6-99.3% identity — including a 28.5 kb block at 182.047-182.076 Mb
    that overlaps the G2 tract interval — and has NO >=90% match to PAN027's
    paternal chr1p subtelomere (first 1.5 Mb), and none to the maternal
    chr5q copy either.
  => PAN028's maternal chr5q subtelomere descends from PAN027's PATERNAL
     chr5q — i.e. a meiotic crossover near the chr5q telomere in PAN027's
     germline put paternal subtelomeric sequence on the transmitted chr5,
     carrying the same standing chr1p-derived tract.

Therefore the recurrence is INHERITANCE, which is the expected signature of
a real transmitted structural feature — not a second independent event and
not a copy-paste artifact. The "it happens twice" argument inverts: an
assembly artifact tied to a shared repeat could also light up in both
scans, but the data show the G3 region is a ~97.7-99.3% match to the G2
tract's host copy on PAN027's paternal chr5q, i.e. one arrangement seen
in two meioses because it was transmitted.

Caveats that keep this honest (and are already in the manuscript):
  - ALIGNER PROVENANCE: the chr5q/chr1p highlight is a SWEEPGA 0.1.1 (FastGA
    f32, 10:10, scaffold-jump 0) -> IMPG 0.4.1 class-winner call — the layer

  - CORRECTED wfmash INTERPRETATION (2026-10-15, direct test): the earlier
    claim "wfmash does not reproduce chr5q/chr1p" is an artifact of the
    DEFAULT filter regime, NOT absent homology. The validation package
    (pedigree_whole_genome_wfmash_p95_updated_bin) ran ONLY literal `-p 95`

    Direct tests on the v1.1 assemblies (2026-10-15):

        identity (e.g. query 182.025-182.038 Mb -> chr1.h2:100-113 kb at

      * What remains a genuine, narrow competition: for the 28 kb f32 core
        (182.052-182.080 Mb) wfmash's best match is the father's chr5 hap2
        at 100% identity — at a position ~200 kb more proximal (the same
        module stands in PAN011's chr5q too) — so chr1p-vs-chr5 margins are
        ~0.1-0.5%. The weak-margin argument stands; the "other aligner
        disagrees" argument does not.
  - MANUSCRIPT CONSEQUENCE TO REVISIT: CROSS_ALIGNER_VALIDATION.md and
    paper.tex currently justify tiering chr5q/chr1p below chr9q/chr3q by
    wfmash non-reproduction. With the corrected interpretation the
    justification should shift to (a) the narrow best-match margin and

  - The f32 chr1p identities (0.998-1.0) vs wfmash chr5q-paternal identities

SCOPE CORRECTION (2026-10-16, later same day): the tie finding above
applies to the chr3q-side INTERNAL patches only. The chr9q-side
TERMINAL blocks — the primary Fig5 events ("chr3q primary-donor
~45 kb" G2 / "~34 kb" G3 per the event manifest) — are a DIFFERENT
story, and there the structure is real (FastGA on v1.1 assemblies,
terminal slices of the telomere-first 500 kb windows):

  G2: PAN027 paternal chr9q terminal ~55 kb matches PAN011 chr3q
      h2 at 99.81% (q 992-60,000) and h1 at 99.63%; the chr9q h2
      HOMOLOG IS ABSENT beyond ~5 kb (only 97-98% fragments at the
      very tip). BUT the same structure STANDS IN PAN011: PAN011's own
      chr9q h2 terminal ~54 kb matches its own chr3q h1/h2 at 99.6%
      with the chr9 h1 homolog absent beyond ~6 kb. => the G2 terminal
      block is INHERITED standing architecture, not a de novo exchange.
  G3: PAN028 chr9q terminal region: the MATERNAL chr9q homolog (the
      homolog of the transmitted body) is ABSENT beyond ~5 kb (base-
      level bins: 98.7% in the first 5 kb, then zero). The tip 6-40 kb
      matches PAN027's PATERNAL chr9q tip colinearly at 99.79%
      (34,039 bp, offset -1,284) — i.e. a telomere-proximal crossover
      transmitted the paternal chr9q tip, which is itself the
      chr3-derived standing block. Per-5 kb bins vs all four PAN027
      chr3/chr9 windows: best matches 98.3-99.96%, alternating between
      paternal chr3q, paternal chr9q and maternal chr3q; NOTHING at
      ~100% — no clean conversion tract datable to the pedigree.
  => Net: Fig5's chr9q<->chr3q ribbons reflect (a) a real standing
     chr3-derived terminal arrangement on the paternal chr9q line
     (present already in PAN011, homolog absent), and (b) ordinary
     telomere-proximal crossover transmission of that tip into
     PAN028's chr9q (with the maternal chr9q homolog absent at the tip
     being the structural signature). NOT two de novo inter-chromosomal
     exchanges. The "happens twice" critique resolves to transmission
     here as well, but via real derived structure rather than rank
     ties. Figures: http://hypervolu.me/~erik/phr/chr9q_terminal_zoom.pdf
     (terminal 100 kb zoom; the maternal-chr9q-absence box measures
     ink 0) and chr3q_chr9q_paternal_focal.pdf (updated boxes).

LINEAGE SCOPE (2026-10-16, rev 5 — PAN010 test): the chr3-derived chr9q
terminal arrangement is NOT private to the paternal line, and the
exchange itself predates the family:

  - PAN011: the ~54 kb chr3-derived tip sits on chr9 HAP2 only; his
    chr9 hap1 tip is a different structure (only ~5 kb 97% fragments
    vs chr3 + one shared 10.5 kb repeat block).
  - PAN010 (grandmother, genetically unrelated to the paternal line):
    her chr9 HAP2 terminal carries the SAME architecture — ~92-98 kb
    matching her own chr3 at 99.65-99.66% (both chr3 haplotypes), with
    her chr9 hap1 matching only the first ~21 kb. Her hap1 tip is a
    different structure. One haplotype each, in BOTH unrelated
    founders.
  - The two carriers' derived blocks also match each other
    (~118 kb sharing between PAN010 chr9:h2 tip and PAN027's paternal
    chr9q tip), consistent with ONE ancestral arrangement shared by
    descent (independent recurrence at a hotspot not excluded).

  => Interpretation for the paper: the chr9q terminus IS ectopically
     derived (chr3q-derived block, chr9 homolog absent at the tip —
     structural, base-level fact, robust to seg-dup polymorphism), but
     the exchange is OLD and population-segregating, present on one
     haplotype in each founder. Within the pedigree everything observed
     is transmission: PAN011 h2 -> PAN027 paternal chr9q -> PAN028
     (telomere-proximal crossover). The Fig5 chr9q/chr3q panels should
     be presented as standing transmitted ectopic-derived architecture
     — an ancestral subtelomeric exchange — not as de novo pedigree
     events. This both matches the data and fully defuses the
     "happens twice" critique: one old exchange, seen in two meioses.
     Figure: http://hypervolu.me/~erik/phr/chr9q_terminal_zoom.pdf
     (13 windows incl. PAN010; maternal-homolog-absence box ink 0).

ROBUSTNESS OF THE ABSENCE CALL (2026-10-16, rev 6): the G3 tip's lack of
maternal-chr9q homology is NOT a multi-mapping/filter artifact. In the
RAW unfiltered FastGA PAF (permissive -f512 seeding, no 10:10 filter, no
length cutoff), PAN028 chr9q tip vs PAN027 maternal chr9q: the ONLY hits
are the first ~5.5 kb of the tip mapping at 96-98% to ~25 positions of
the maternal chr9q terminal 100 kb (a multi-copy subtelomeric repeat);
zero alignments of ANY length over telomere-first positions ~6-33 kb.
The paternal chr9q tip matches the same region colinearly at 99.79%
over 34 kb (offset -1,284). => parent-of-origin structure (maternal
body + paternal tip crossover) is filter-depth-independent; only the
block-level chr3-vs-chr9 attribution inside the standing segdup cloud
is depth-sensitive (near-tie templates).
