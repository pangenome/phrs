R2. "The chr3q<->9q ectopic crossover recurs in two generations (PAN027 and
    PAN028); it happens twice, so there must be some problem with it."
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STATUS: OPEN — under active analysis (do not treat as an artifact yet).

The two observations in question (from all_pedigrees_patches.tsv, both
crossover_like, both community C3, both flagger/problematic-overlap = 0):

  G2: PAN027 paternal (hap2) vs PAN011 (father)
      chr3q flank 345,337-348,751 (3,414 bp), source chr9q:h2 (PAN011),
      context chr3q:h2 (L) / chr3q:h1 (R), min_score 1.0000
      asm: PAN027.chr3.paternal:202,393,547-202,396,961

  G3: PAN028 maternal (hap1) vs PAN027 (mother)
      chr3q flank 366,221-368,310 (2,089 bp), source chr9q:h2 (PAN027),
      context chr3q:h2 (L) / chr3q:h1 (R), min_score 0.9701
      asm: PAN028.chr3.haplotype1:199,599,977-199,602,066
      (a second, larger chr3q crossover-like patch 262,953-290,922,
      27,969 bp, source chr7p:h2, min_score 0.9432, also flagger-negative)

Untangle detail (PAN027_vs_PAN011.e50000.m1000.bed.gz): the G2 tract maps to
PAN011#2#chr9 (hap2) at identity 1.0, nth.best=1, self.cov~1.27 — i.e. in
that window the chr9q hap2 match is the BEST target genome-wide, beating
both of the father's chr3q haplotypes; flanking segments prefer chr3q:h2
(left) / chr3q:h1 (right). chr3q and chr9q subtelomeres share extensive
homology (Leiden C3), so long stretches of the chr3q flank also align to
chr9q:h2 at 0.82-0.999 but with nth.best>1 (chr3 targets win) — only the
3.4 kb tract is a genome-wide best hit.

Key observation so far: the two instances are NOT identical.
  - Different positions in the flank (345,337 vs 366,221 from the q-telomere;
    different assembly intervals).
  - Different identity to their respective chr9q:h2 sources (1.0000 vs
    0.9701). A shared/copy-pasted assembly artifact or a transmitted copy of
    the same tract would be ~identical to the source; two different
    tract sequences at slightly different positions is the signature of two
    INDEPENDENT events at the same subtelomeric hotspot — the opposite of
    the artifact argument.

Hypotheses to distinguish (next steps):
  (a) transmission of a standing rearrangement (PAN028 inherits PAN027's
      paternal chr3q subtelomere wholesale): predicts the PAN028 tract is
      the SAME sequence as the PAN027 tract, and untangle of PAN028 vs
      PAN027 should map it to chr3q:h2 (which contains the older copy), not
      chr9q:h2.
  (b) independent recurrent events at a chr3q/9q (C3) hotspot: predicts the
      two tracts differ (already suggested by the 1.0000 vs 0.9701 scores)
      and use possibly different chr9q source intervals.
  (c) assembly artifact: predicts identical tracts/junctions in both
      assemblies and/or flagger/nucflag signal — currently contradicted by
      the differing identities, differing positions, and 0 bp problematic
      overlap for both instances (and by the same argument, a 'switch
      error' origin — cf. Monika's sw_prone_regions.bed — would need to be
      checked; note both tracts are flagger-negative but switch-error
      prone regions are a separate track worth intersecting too).
